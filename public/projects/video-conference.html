Key takeaway: In this historical review sample, privacy had the lowest theme-level sentiment score, while microphones had the lowest score among the categories listed in the thesis. Together with the review content, these findings suggest prioritizing microphone and camera controls and improving the reliability of existing features.

Why this research?

App-store ratings indicate how users feel, but they do not explain which experiences cause frustration. This study analyzes Chinese-language reviews of Tencent Meeting to identify user concerns and inform product improvements.

The research addresses three questions:

What aspects of video conferencing do users discuss?

How does expressed sentiment differ across themes and feature categories?

Which issues warrant further investigation and improvement?

Research approach

1. Clean and prepare the reviews

Remove duplicate, empty, and uninformative content; normalize Chinese text; then apply jieba word segmentation and stop-word removal to prepare the corpus.

2. Identify themes

Use TF-IDF to identify salient textual features and topic coherence to select the number of LDA topics. The researcher interprets the resulting topics as four themes:

App functionality: feature experiences, permissions, and external-device use.

App performance: network connectivity, lag, and power consumption.

Privacy: microphones, cameras, private messaging, and related concerns.

Usage context: classes, work, examinations, and other settings.

3. Extract features and sentiment

Build feature and sentiment dictionaries to extract word pairs from sentences. When direct extraction is unresolved, use Doc2Vec to find similar sentences and assist with feature or sentiment labeling.

4. Aggregate sentiment scores

Use retrained SnowNLP models to score sentiment words and sentences. Apply adjustments for negation and intensity, then use TF-IDF weights to aggregate scores by theme and category.

Reading the scores: These are weighted sentiment scores produced by the studyʼs method, not satisfaction percentages or problem incidence rates. The adjusted underlying scores can reach 2, so 0.5 should not be treated as a calibrated boundary between satisfaction and dissatisfaction. The findings below emphasize the reported descriptive rankings.

Key findings

Privacy has the lowest theme score

Theme Sentiment score
Privacy 0.476
App performance 0.554
App functionality 0.574
Usage context 0.579

Privacy has the lowest score among the four themes. Drawing on the review content, the thesis connects this result to unexpected microphone or camera activation and concerns about losing control over personal information.

These differences are descriptive. The thesis does not provide corresponding confidence intervals or significance tests, so the results do not establish statistically significant differences between themes.

Microphones have the lowest listed category score

The following are the six lowest-scoring categories listed in Table 5.7:

Microphones 0.409

Microphone and camera controls emerge as experiences worth investigating. Permission settings, power consumption, and external-device use, including casting, also receive relatively low scores.

The online-class category suggests that some negative experiences may relate to the usage context, such as prolonged online classes. These experiences cannot all be attributed to software defects.

All values above come from Table 5.7. A categoryʼs score does not indicate how frequently the issue was mentioned or the proportion of users affected.

Implications for product design

The following directions are proposed in the thesis. Their effects have not been validated through intervention experiments.

Strengthen microphone and camera controls

Investigate and address unexpected activation and other control failures.

Consider a microphone lock mode to reduce unintended activation.

Explore default settings suited to different usage contexts.

Improve the reliability of existing features

Investigate power consumption and optimize resource use.

Improve casting compatibility and stability.

Clarify permissions and provide accessible casting guidance.

Help users recognize meeting burden

Consider daily meeting-duration tracking and reminders.

Help users and organizers identify prolonged or excessive online meetings.

Treat duration feedback as a supporting measure; it cannot independently resolve burdens imposed by class or work schedules.

Limitations and unresolved issues

Scope of the findings: The data covers reviews from selected platforms during 2022–2023. It does not necessarily represent all users or the current version of Tencent Meeting.

Sentiment interpretation: Sarcasm, mismatches between star ratings and review text, and dictionary-based classification may affect the results. Assigning a single feature to a sentence can also obscure multiple concerns.

No independent replication: This summary is based on the thesis and the poster package prepared from it. The original review dataset and analysis code were not available for this work, and the research models were not rerun.

Inconsistent reporting: The methods describe 30 subcategories, but Table 5.7 lists 29. Some counts reported during feature extraction also do not fully reconcile. This page preserves explicitly reported values without inferring missing processing steps.

Recommendations are not demonstrated outcomes: The findings support treating these issues as candidates for product investigation and improvement. They do not establish that the proposed changes would increase retention, satisfaction, or meeting efficiency.
